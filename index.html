The Method

One sentence stating your point beats a phrase headline every time.

Michael Alley developed the Assertion-Evidence method at Penn State. I contributed to his research as a student and co-author, and remain a practitioner of it today.

Assertion

The headline is a full sentence stating the one claim worth remembering.

Evidence

The body shows proof: a chart, image, or diagram, instead of restating the claim in text.

Learn more from Michael Alley directly through his website, assertion-evidence.com or his book, The Craft of Scientific Presentations.
